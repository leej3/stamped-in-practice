# Trial with failed results reporting

This completed development trial tested the research-and-assessment workflow before the larger NARPS study runs.
Twelve research workers and sixteen assigned assessments finished.
Reporting and assessment problems, together with workflow requirements that constrained the workers, made this a trial of the experimental setup rather than a clean test of skill improvement.
The name describes that reporting experience; it does not mean every worker's scientific result was wrong.

## What to read

- [Findings and figures](replay/RESULTS.md) describe what could be learned from the retained work.

The planned three-round design was not completed as one comparable series.
The completed first round became development evidence, and subsequent work used a separately registered design.
Read the [project's trial history](https://github.com/leej3/stamped-in-practice/blob/main/docs/trials.md) for the relationship between the two efforts and the [project-wide lessons](https://github.com/leej3/stamped-in-practice/blob/main/LESSONS.md) for what should change next.
