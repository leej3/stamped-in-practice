# Analysis protocol (fixed before group inference)

- **Question and unit:** Does larger potential gain predict greater response in vmPFC for the equal-indifference group? Unit of independent sampling is participant (54 participants); each contributes one AO86 gain-effect map averaged across four runs.
- **Estimand:** Group mean of the participant gain-modulator coefficient at each voxel. The gain regressor was centered and variance-normalized before HRF convolution. Coefficients are not per-currency slopes and are conditional on the separate gain/loss upstream models.
- **Primary test:** One-sided positive one-sample group test, voxelwise t statistic, sign-flip permutation max-t familywise correction over the common brain support (5,000 random sign flips; seed 20261016; alpha .05). This directional test follows Hypothesis 1. Equal-weight participant effects are used because subject variance maps are unavailable.
- **Spatial domain and localization:** Common support is voxels nonzero in every map; zero-valued out-of-mask voxels are not treated as measured zero. vmPFC is interpreted anatomically as medial, anterior, ventral prefrontal cortex (including medial orbitofrontal/subcallosal territory). Whole-brain corrected significant clusters, with MNI coordinates and their anatomical position, determine whether evidence is in vmPFC; no uncorrected ROI result can establish the hypothesis.
- **QC and exclusions:** Check complete ID match, duplicate IDs, image readability, common shape/affine, finite values, and common support. Exclude only a participant with a documented file/geometry/nonfinite failure. No intensity-based exclusions or covariate selection. Report any departures.
- **Sensitivity/validity:** Compare parametric one-sample t results at the observed peak with the permutation result; check influence descriptively via participant map/ROI distribution and report uncertainty. Do not select inference by significance.
- **Limits:** Inference is conditional on fixed upstream processing; no time series or participant variance maps are supplied. The group model cannot propagate first-level uncertainty or test alternate gain/loss models. Sign-flip inference assumes participant effects are exchangeable and approximately symmetric under the null.
