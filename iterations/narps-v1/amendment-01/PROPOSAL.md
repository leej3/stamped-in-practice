Status: prepared for independent review; not approved, sealed or active.
No new formal launches are authorized by this file.

The target retained locked annex pointers; it had not fetched those payloads.

Its receipt records host-assisted access on the same host; successful replay does not establish anonymous access, publication discoverability or cross-host portability.

## Two immutable identities

The original `procedure.json`, its digest, allocation, six research launches, scientific result commits and worker-stop receipts stay unchanged.
The proposed assessment-only seal embeds the original seal's exact JSON bytes and SHA-256.
It separately pins the corrected assessor and executing assessment adapter.
Only `assessment-version.json` and the three named source-validation/launch adapters may differ in the fixed-file boundary. All other fixed files, study inputs and guidance, prompts, conditions, resources, runtime/model/settings, starting environment, replay limits and module source configuration must match.

The new seal cannot launch research.
Research continues through the original frozen driver and original seal; subsequent research bases can change the tested skills gitlink as originally allowed.
Every condition in a round uses one matched research source revision.
The assessment adapter verifies each worker's original committed source against the original research seal, and verifies its own executing files against the new assessment seal.
It never rewrites a worker input seed or scientific history to satisfy a new check.
`retained-objects.json` additionally fixes the six existing run, seed, result and original stop-receipt identities; relabeling a retained object is rejected.

## Disposition and interpretation

Both original judgment attempts remain byte-exact under their original seal.
They are infrastructure attempts excluded from the resumed primary and duplicate outcome comparison, irrespective of their reported scientific categories or checklist judgments.
Their work, costs and failures remain operational evidence.
Every formal object will receive the same corrected assessment; the first assigned assessment under that common corrected procedure is primary, with the same preselected duplicate allocations.
No favorable judgment is selected from competing versions.

Aggregation must validate each included judgment's retained native launch/procedure receipt against the corrected assessment digest, original research digest, exact target result and original worker-stop receipt.
A valid checklist response alone is insufficient: old and corrected assessors share checklist identifiers.
Mixed-version or mismatched identities must be rejected before figures and outcome summaries are produced.

The judging prompt omits condition labels and coordinator verdicts, but the exact research object includes `worker-input.json`, which exposes condition and assignment metadata.
The original design therefore does not guarantee condition blinding.
No assessed object is redacted or altered to conceal this limitation.

Remaining gates: independent review of the concrete code and proofs, complete regression results, publication of the corrected assessor commit, explicit amendment decision, new immutable seal and inclusion-gate validation.
No existing assessment result is repaired or rerun in place.
