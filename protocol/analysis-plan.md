# Analysis plan: NARPS Hypothesis 1

## Question and population
Test whether gain-effect estimates are positive at the group level in the NARPS equal-indifference cohort (54 participants, one map per participant). The independent sampling unit is the participant. No exclusions are planned; exclusions will be based only on absent/corrupt maps, incompatible geometry, or nonfinite data and will be reported with reasons.

## Estimand and input interpretation
The target is the population mean participant-level gain contrast at each voxel (one-sample intercept). Maps are the supplied unthresholded participant contrast parameter estimates (COPE-like effect estimates), averaged across four runs, in MNI152NLin2009cAsym. They are neither standardized statistics nor per-unit-gain slopes. Upstream gain modulators were centered and variance-normalized before HRF convolution; therefore the contrast reflects response to the normalized gain modulator and is not a change per currency unit. Since gains and losses were modeled separately, interpretation is conditional on that upstream modeling choice. Upstream participant variance maps are unavailable, so group inference treats participant effect estimates as equally precise and cannot implement inverse-variance weighting or validate first-level standard errors.

## Primary model and inference
At each in-brain voxel, fit a one-sample intercept-only model to the 54 participant effects and test the directional alternative mean > 0. The primary statistic is the one-sample t statistic. Control whole-brain familywise error by a one-sided maximum-t sign-flip permutation test (9,999 random participant-level sign flips; fixed seed 20261015; plus-one corrected p values). Use a 2-mm MNI152 standard brain mask restricted to voxels with finite observations in all included maps. Sign flipping assumes independent participants and sign-symmetric null errors; this provides a nonparametric reference for the studentized statistic but does not cure upstream estimation error or selection. Alpha is .05. No ROI-only significance claim will be used. For the hypothesis-specific localization, any familywise-significant positive cluster/voxel must anatomically overlap ventromedial prefrontal cortex; report coordinates and inspect unthresholded and corrected maps in MNI views. vmPFC is a broad anatomical description, not a formal a priori voxel mask supplied by NARPS; localization will be conservative and descriptive.

## Checks and sensitivity
Check participant IDs against the cohort table and map manifest; duplicate/missing IDs; input checksums; image shape, affine, voxel size, orientation, finite values, brain-mask coverage and per-map intensity distribution. Report QC flags separately from unique exclusions. As a sensitivity analysis, report parametric one-sample t values and uncorrected/Bonferroni voxelwise p values on the same mask, plus ROI-free whole-brain unthresholded maps. This is a method check, not an alternate route to significance. Summarize effect magnitudes and participant-level dispersion, with uncertainty for the full-brain average and selected vmPFC peak labeled as descriptive (peak selection is not independent).

## Outputs and stopping rule
Save full unthresholded t and mean-effect maps, corrected p map, thresholded positive map, QC and summary tables, a participant mean distribution/diagnostic figure, and an anatomical statistical-map figure. Support requires at least one positive familywise-corrected voxel in vmPFC at alpha .05. Failure to reject means evidence is insufficient for a positive group effect under this model and supplied contrast; it does not establish no effect, no coding of gain, or equivalence to zero.

For descriptive visualization only, define a broad coordinate box as |x| ≤ 10 mm, 20 ≤ y ≤ 60 mm, and −20 ≤ z ≤ 20 mm in MNI space. It is intended to summarize a ventral medial prefrontal swath, not to represent a validated atlas ROI; only voxels passing whole-brain max-t correction can support the hypothesis, and their anatomical locations will be checked against this region and shown in orthogonal views. A peak within the box does not alone establish a regional effect.
