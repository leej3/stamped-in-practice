## Scientific assessment

The 54 maps represent independent participants’ unthresholded gain-effect estimates averaged across runs, not t/z statistics; their upstream gain regressor was variance-normalized and cannot be interpreted per currency unit. The participant-level one-sample t model is appropriate to these inputs given the absent within-participant variance maps, subject to the stated assumptions about independent participants and between-person effects. The code checks all 54 files against the source index, verifies common dimensions and affine, and reports finite-value and coverage checks. It intersects nonzero coverage across participants and a resampled symmetric ICBM 2009a mask on the supplied asymmetric 2009c grid. This is a defensible practical mask with a disclosed edge mismatch; treating zeros as absent coverage could also exclude true zero estimates at individual voxels, though this is unlikely to drive a vmPFC-wide conclusion.

The AAL medial orbital frontal plus rectus region is a defensible, broad vmPFC proxy, not a unique anatomical definition. Its unadjusted summary is descriptive; the hypothesis claim is assessed using whole-brain corrected inference. The reported ROI mean is −0.0036 map units (95% CI −0.0285 to 0.0212; one-sided unadjusted p=.615; n=54). Eleven voxels pass positive one-sided Bonferroni FWE across 186,611 tested voxels, in three posterior occipital foci, with no overlap in the ROI. This supports “no evidence for the predicted positive vmPFC effect” in this analysis, not equivalence to zero, no brain response, or absence under another upstream model. The Bonferroni procedure is conservative and the upstream model uncertainty remains.
