It reports effect scale limitations, missing first-level variance maps, uncertainty, and the distinction between no evidence and evidence of exact zero. The script forms its analysis mask from nonzero support in at least 90% of participants and then analyzes the resulting values, including zeros for any participant with zero-valued voxels. The report does not show the participant-by-voxel coverage pattern or demonstrate that such zeros always mean a measured zero rather than absent image support. I could not independently inspect the NIfTI voxel arrays in the frozen assessor environment. This leaves uncertainty about the mask and the ROI estimates, but the available evidence does not establish a material error or justify rejecting the conclusion. Participant variance maps and the upstream model are absent as stated in the fixed-input methods, so the inference remains conditional on supplied first-level estimates and their assumptions.

The retained result summary reports 10,000 sign flips, 210,968 mask voxels, no FWE-significant vmPFC voxels, and 24 positive FWE-significant voxels outside vmPFC (peak at MNI [−26, −102, −3.6]).
