# What this comparison can teach us

These lessons describe the original frozen extraction.
The [subsequent authorized reporting correction](REPORTING-CORRECTION.md) addresses the source-binding admission issue separately; the original rejection records and individual failures remain unchanged.

The main measurement failure occurred between rounds.
The launcher accepted the registered change to the research-skill source, but the frozen aggregator still required the original coordinator commit.
All 32 judgments in each of rounds two and three were consequently rejected before later admission checks.
Successful agent execution and preserved output did not establish that the comparison could be scored.
A future rehearsal needs to exercise a complete skill revision between two rounds, through final aggregation, before freezing the procedure.
The provenance contract needs to distinguish fixed scientific and assessment inputs from the explicitly registered skill revision allowed to change between rounds.
This is a lesson from the observed failure, not a repair applied to this series.

Assessment reliability is a separate limitation.
In the retained round-one reassessment, duplicate judges disagreed on 67 of 248 checklist decisions and on scientific adequacy for five of eight research objects.
In the documented round-one judgment `04-j01`, displaying the supplied procedure exposed the worker's treatment assignment.
Keeping an assessor fixed makes its behavior traceable; it does not establish reliability or successful blinding.
Later missing scores cannot be treated as agreement, failure of the research, or evidence that skills helped.

Skill instructions and delivered artifacts need separate evidence.
All six explicit-skill workers in round two read the supplied workflow, while five inspected setup documents still relied on the shared host runtime and version information.
One worker delivered an environment manifest.
The [inspected documents](../skill-reflection/round-03-research-guidance-01/delivery-evidence.json) retain exact paths, copies and hashes.
The single round-three clarification asks for the recipe itself or a retrievable pinned source alongside the runtime identity.
These file observations justify testing clearer wording; they do not establish a causal skill benefit.
Installation, reading instructions, executing a helper and obtaining a valid scientific result are different observations.

Isolation was limited by the shared host.
A round-two discovery worker read a user-level PDF skill outside its supplied profile.
Its actual exposure is retained rather than silently relabeling its condition.
Similarly, the increase from three to ten concurrent sessions during round two is part of the execution record.
Session durations remain descriptive and cannot by themselves establish an efficiency advantage.

The retained research objects, native records, exact skill revisions, rejected judgments and figures remain useful evidence.
Local archive verification and published Git references are reported separately from remote annex retrieval; pending payload uploads are not described as complete publication.
This series does not establish that iterative STAMPED guidance improves scientific performance, reduces variability or transfers to another study.
