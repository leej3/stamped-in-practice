The exact deltas are restricted to `.git/annex/keysdb/db` and its `db-shm`/`db-wal` sidecars.

Its exact receipt is retained under `../coordinator-validation/round-closure-01/round-01-synthesis-root-review-01/`.

The decision is to preserve and report the original series in full, including its attrition, and to hold subsequent research for a bounded measurement-viability decision.
A prospective implementation separates the immutable canonical delivery from an operational inspection copy, retains all operational deltas, and adds a structured scientific record without changing the scientific categories.
It remains a candidate until a live synthetic trial and explicit registration decision establish its scope.
Engineering evidence is not an additional NARPS judgment.

If adopted, the repaired assessment must cover all 24 existing objects and the same eight preselected duplicates in a separate series.
No failed-only retry, best-of selection, relabeling of duplicate roles, or silent pooling with this original series is allowed.
Changing the measurement procedure creates a comparability boundary even if the scientific criteria remain the same.
An apparent improvement in availability after repair is evidence about the instrument, not about improved research skills.
