## Scientific assessment

Reported hypothesis outcome: not supported by corrected positive vmPFC evidence.** The conclusion in `report.md` is appropriately limited to lack of corrected evidence, not proof of zero effect. The reported vmPFC mean is −0.00051 map-estimate units (95% descriptive t interval −0.0222 to 0.0212), and no vmPFC voxel survives positive one-sided whole-brain max-T FWE correction. The 24 FWE-significant positive voxels are posterior occipital, not vmPFC (`results/summary.json`, `results/fwe_clusters.csv`, `results/top_voxels.csv`).

The analysis uses 54 independent participant maps, checks source checksums and geometry, excludes nobody, defines a common nonzero coverage mask, and reports input QC (`analysis/group_analysis.py`, `results/input_qc.csv`). A one-sample voxelwise t statistic with participant-level whole-map sign flips and max-T correction across the full analyzed coverage is a defensible whole-brain procedure under independent participants and a symmetric null. The directional positive test matches the stated hypothesis. The ROI is defined transparently with Harvard–Oxford labels, nearest-neighbour resampling, MNI restrictions, and intersection with common coverage (`results/vmPFC_mask_definition.txt`). Participant-level ROI summaries and their interval avoid voxel pseudoreplication (`results/vmPFC_participant_effects.csv`, figure 2).

The study task and `input/upstream-methods.md` make clear that the supplied maps are normalized gain coefficients conditional on fixed upstream models, not effects per currency unit; participant variance maps and the executable upstream pipeline are absent. The report states these limits, the sign-flip symmetry assumption, anatomical ambiguity, and the interpretation of a null result. The figures make the localization and participant-level dispersion inspectable. Small caveats do not overturn the central inference: nonzero coverage is used as the common analysis mask rather than a separately supplied anatomical mask; the atlas template differs from the effect-map template; and permutation p-values are Monte Carlo estimates from 10,000 draws. These choices are disclosed and do not create an evident material inference error.

**Scientific evidence attainment:** adequate group analysis, QC, correction, anatomical localization, uncertainty and interpretation are present. **Replay measurement:** not checked; this is separate from the scientific assessment and does not invalidate the shipped evidence.
