## Scientific assessment

**Scientific adequacy: supported.** This label supports the report’s conclusion that the supplied data do not establish the predicted positive vmPFC response; it does not assert that the true effect is zero. The independent analysis uses all 54 equal-indifference participants, checks IDs, checksums, geometry and finite coverage, and excludes no participants. An unweighted one-sample group t model is defensible because the supplied maps are participant effect estimates and participant variance maps are absent. Its one-sided sign-flip max-T procedure addresses whole-brain FWE, with the symmetry assumption stated. The bilateral AAL3 medial-orbitofrontal proxy is a defensible, explicit vmPFC proxy, and the ROI is not used to bypass whole-brain correction.

**Reported hypothesis outcome: not supported.** The vmPFC proxy mean is −0.00795 supplied units (descriptive 95% t interval −0.03455 to 0.01864), with zero voxels surviving whole-brain correction in the proxy (`summary.json`). The corrected map has 23 positive voxels in three small posterior-inferior foci, outside the vmPFC proxy; the report does not interpret them as gain-coding evidence. Because the upstream gain regressor was normalized, coefficients are not changes per currency unit. The interval is not an equivalence test, and this analysis cannot revisit upstream modeling or establish causality. These limits make “no corrected evidence here” more defensible than “no neural effect.”
