## Scientific judgment

**Scientific adequacy: qualified.** The delivered analysis supports the limited conclusion that it did not establish a positive vmPFC gain effect under its stated group model, anatomical definition and whole-brain correction. The one-sided sign-flip max-T analysis uses 54 independent participant maps, 4,999 sign flips and a 210,968-voxel support mask; it reports 159 FWER-corrected positive voxels and none in the vmPFC mask. The ROI mean is −0.003 map units (95% t interval −0.028 to 0.022; t(53)=−0.24; descriptive one-sided p=.595). The analysis correctly treats these estimates as participant-model coefficients rather than BOLD change per dollar and notes that participant variance maps are unavailable.

Qualification: the code defines support from nonzero coverage at 49 or more participants, then includes the full set of 54 values—including zeros at any partially covered locations—in voxelwise means, standard errors and permutations. No missingness sensitivity is reported. The vmPFC mask is independently defined but transferred between MNI152 template variants, so its anatomical localization is approximate; the worker discloses this. These limitations do not turn the result into evidence of equivalence or absence. The supplied first-level maps and upstream model remain fixed inputs.

**Hypothesis conclusion (worker’s verbatim reported outcome):** “This group analysis does not support a positive gain effect in vmPFC.” The lack of corrected vmPFC voxels is the reported outcome; it is not a positive-hypothesis finding. Scientific adequacy, the hypothesis outcome, checklist attainment and replay measurement availability are separate judgments.
