## Scientific judgment

Scientific adequacy: supported.

Hypothesis conclusion (verbatim from the worker report): “In the 54 equal-indifference participants, the supplied gain-effect maps do not support a positive vmPFC response at the required whole-brain-corrected level.”

The reported conclusion follows from a defensible group analysis of the supplied fixed participant estimates. The worker reports checking all 54 participant maps against the source checksums, finite values, and common image geometry; it excludes no participant. A one-sided one-sample voxelwise *t* test across participants was evaluated over the intersection of nonzero map support (198,029 voxels) with Bonferroni whole-brain familywise correction. The maximum positive statistic was *t*(53)=6.62, with corrected *p*=0.00184, but the 11 corrected voxels were in three occipital clusters and none overlapped the predeclared broad vmPFC proxy. This distinction is appropriate: a corrected effect elsewhere does not support the vmPFC hypothesis.

The Harvard–Oxford Frontal Medial, Subcallosal, and Frontal Orbital union is a transparent, broad vmPFC proxy rather than a uniquely canonical region. Its mean estimate is −0.0048 with a 95% CI of [−0.0243, 0.0147]; the unadjusted ROI-only one-sided *p*=0.690 is appropriately labeled secondary and is not treated as the confirmatory test. The participant-effect and whole-brain figures make the result inspectable. The underlying report and results are canonical `content/workspace/report.md` (SHA-256 `47cb2e5c82aaf7dacd5c21a367c8a6069a0a6fff6dc58a37da7cbd6f0f9a42da`), `content/workspace/results/results.json` (`647a39f521e1465ae7ba241070cc8c043c967433988df21fa40499b617a46668`), `content/workspace/results/whole_brain_summary.tsv` (`088642cce63d7394dff359e03e5b21895e0781a55633a4169b9097c0bbed3121`), `content/workspace/results/whole_brain_fwe_clusters.tsv` (`8a89faeab3e66edd4aad43ef2e7ab8451130a020e06b50829acf0a6bc2c6185c`), and figures `content/workspace/results/figures/group_t_unthresholded_with_vmPFC.png` (`8348f28f2d251af28b269feae097b53770929897405c247e7e53d67083039c56`) and `content/workspace/results/figures/positive_fwe_thresholded.png` (`836b5f810d8b7e69933664dd068b9a1e6443e5bf5466cbc4345d264d91415c0b`), all cited under canonical capture `35c629…3048`.

The interpretation is appropriately bounded. These are unthresholded contrast parameter estimates from a mean-centered, variance-normalized gain regressor, not effects per currency unit; participant variance maps are absent, so precision-weighted group inference was unavailable. The one-sample *t* model relies on independent participants and the usual group-level distribution assumptions. The common nonzero-support mask assumes zeros denote outside-support voxels. The ROI is atlas-dependent, Bonferroni is conservative, and failure to find corrected vmPFC evidence is not evidence of a zero effect. These are limitations, not material errors in the stated conclusion.
