Science uses primary judgments only. Pending means the assigned primary judgment has not closed; unavailable means a closed observation has no extractable category. These availability states are separate from the judge's category `unable to assess`. Duplicates do not replace primary judgments.

Effort is elapsed native-session wall time, grouped exactly as in the validated operation table. It includes session work and waiting; computation-only time is not inferred. Durations are not summed, and no billing inference is made. Original research effort belongs to the closed original deliveries, while judgment effort belongs to this new series.
