## Scientific assessment

**Scientific adequacy: supported.** The report’s conclusion is supported by a defensible analysis conditional on the supplied participant models. All 54 participants were included; the script checks source byte counts and SHA-256 values, finite voxel data, identical dimensions and affines, and uses the intersection of nonzero coverage. The primary one-sided sign-flip test controls voxelwise whole-brain FWE by the maximum studentized statistic over 198,029 common-coverage voxels. The stated null requires participant-level symmetry; equal weighting is appropriate to the supplied copes because participant variance maps are absent. The Harvard–Oxford composite is an explicit, broad anatomical approximation, used descriptively while whole-brain inference remains primary.

The results give a vmPFC mean cope of 0.00265 (95% t interval −0.0217 to 0.0270), one-sided uncorrected p=.414, and no vmPFC voxel surviving whole-brain correction (minimum vmPFC FWE p=.780). Twenty-four corrected positive voxels form three small occipital clusters. The report does not interpret those clusters as vmPFC evidence. It states that failure to reject is not evidence of an exact zero, notes that the interval allows small effects, and does not claim equivalence. This supports the reported absence of corrected positive vmPFC evidence without establishing a null response.

**Hypothesis outcome, quoted verbatim from the scientific report:**

> In the equal-indifference group, these maps do not provide whole-brain-corrected evidence that the gain effect is positive in vmPFC.

The estimates are not per-currency-unit slopes: upstream gain modulation was mean-centered and variance-normalized, and gain/loss were modeled separately. Findings are therefore conditional on the supplied upstream model. The common mask’s nonzero rule assumes zero-valued voxels indicate unavailable coverage; it is conservative if some in-coverage true estimates are exactly zero. This does not alter the report’s cautious interpretation. No task result should be generalized to a monetary-unit dose response.
