**Hypothesis outcome (verbatim from the worker report):** In the 54-person NARPS equal-indifference group, these data do **not support a positive vmPFC gain effect**.

The design is appropriate for the requested group stage. Input hashes and participant IDs are checked; dimensions and affines are required to match; all 54 maps must be finite; and the common nonzero support ensures tested voxels have observations from every participant. The sign-flip max-*t* statistic supplies the required whole-brain correction under the stated independent-participant and symmetric-error assumptions. The vmPFC parcel summary is secondary and anatomically explicit, not substituted for corrected whole-brain inference. The report correctly limits generalization to these upstream estimates and does not interpret a non-significant vmPFC test as equivalence or absence.
