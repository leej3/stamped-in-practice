All 54 participant IDs and image checksums are checked by the script; geometry and finite values are checked; no one was excluded. An assessor-side read-only coverage check found 5,279 of 5,292 voxels in the declared vmPFC box nonzero in every map; the other 13 had 53/54 coverage. The wider 90%-coverage analysis mask includes some voxels with one or more zero-valued observations, which can represent limited field-of-view coverage; this is a modest edge-coverage caveat, not material to the near-complete vmPFC box. The worker acknowledges that participant variance maps are unavailable and that the sign-flip test relies on symmetry/exchangeability. The broad coordinate box is a transparent descriptive proxy, not an independently selected ROI. The report appropriately avoids interpreting the estimates as response per dollar and says that lack of support does not establish no effect. No atlas or particular model is required here.
