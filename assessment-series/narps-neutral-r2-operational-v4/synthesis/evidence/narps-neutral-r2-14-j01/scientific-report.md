## Scientific assessment

The report evaluates the intended 54 equal-indifference participants with a one-sided one-sample test of independent participant gain-effect maps. It uses 4,999 random sign flips and a positive maximum-*t* statistic over the 90%-coverage mask, giving whole-brain family-wise correction. Its vmPFC locator is a prespecified bilateral AAL3v2 `Frontal_Med_Orb` anatomical proxy intersected with that mask. The supplied summary reports 1,306 proxy voxels, no proxy voxels surviving correction, minimum proxy FWE *p*=1.0, and three small corrected clusters outside the proxy. The report does not treat those off-target findings as support for the hypothesis. (`REPORT.md`; `code/group_analysis.py`; `results/summary.json`; `results/fwe_clusters.tsv`.)

The spatial checks are useful: code compares participant IDs to metadata and the source manifest, checks hashes and byte counts, tests finite values and common grids/affines, and the delivered QC tables cover all participants. An assessor-side read-only NIfTI audit found 9 of 1,306 proxy voxels had fewer than 54 nonzero maps, with at most six zero-filled participants at any such proxy voxel; 12,939 of 210,968 whole-brain-mask voxels had incomplete nonzero coverage. The ROI incompleteness is small, but code treats zero-filled missing coverage as an observed coefficient rather than a missing value. The mask and ROI conclusions should therefore be read as conditional on this coverage handling. The exact inspection output is retained in `commands/nifti-coverage.stdout.txt`.

The effect estimates are upstream coefficients for a mean-centered, variance-normalized gain modulator, not currency-unit slopes or raw BOLD change. The report states that gain and loss were modeled separately, and correctly limits inference to a group mean conditional on the supplied first-level model. Equal participant weighting is defensible because participant variance maps are absent; the sign-flip test still relies on independent participants and sign symmetry. The descriptive ROI interval crosses zero and is not substituted for the required whole-brain-corrected test. The AAL3 medial-orbitofrontal parcel is a defensible but narrow proxy for vmPFC. The reported absence of corrected support is supported under those choices, but it does not establish no effect under every vmPFC definition or a true zero response.

Scientific artifacts are coherent as delivered, but no replay was performed: the report points to a host-specific Python installation that is not captured, no environment specification is supplied, and the entrypoint fetches atlas/template resources at runtime. These are reproducibility limits, not evidence against the result. Replay status, exact command, output selection, and comparison rule are recorded in [`replay-record.json`](replay-record.json).
