Its external source is [`9852d836`](https://github.com/con/skills/commit/9852d8360f9519d21d11099b45f5deb8ba80cb28), separate from the evolving research bundle.
All original NARPS judgments remain unchanged.

The closed archives preserve both successful checks and failures, exact supplied snapshots, prompts, delivered files and native sessions.
Their [independent publication check](../migration/reflection-publication-check/result-complete.json) retrieved all new annex payloads and verified both archive manifests and every archived member.
These development checks establish bounded followability and preservation evidence; they do not establish that the skills improve NARPS performance.
The next comparison tests that question with new workers under a stable, declared tool-neutral setup.
