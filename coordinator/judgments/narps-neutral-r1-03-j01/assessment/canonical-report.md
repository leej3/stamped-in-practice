## Scientific assessment

**Scientific adequacy: supported.** The group analysis is an appropriate and transparent test conditional on the supplied AO86 participant estimates. `analysis/group_analysis.py` checks for 54 maps and cohort rows, participant identity/group, finite values, matching shape and affine, and analyzes the common nonzero support. It uses one independent map per participant and a one-sided positive one-sample t test, with Bonferroni familywise correction over all 198,029 supported voxels. Bonferroni is conservative but valid under arbitrary spatial dependence. The report explains that the maps are gain-modulator coefficients normalized upstream, not BOLD change per unit currency, and that participant-level variance maps are absent. The 12-mm MNI sphere at (0, 40, −10) is stated as an operational anatomical summary; whole-brain corrected inference remains primary. These choices address the supplied question without requiring a particular atlas or model.

**Reported hypothesis outcome: no corrected vmPFC support. Scientific conclusion: qualified.** The ROI mean is −0.0055 cope units (95% CI −0.0309 to 0.0199; one-sided *t*(53)=−0.44, *p*=.667), with zero voxels in the ROI surviving whole-brain correction. Eleven positive voxels survive whole-brain Bonferroni correction, but the strongest is posterior at MNI (−26, −102, −3.6), outside the hypothesized region. The report correctly says this is a lack of support, not proof of no effect; its interval is compatible with small effects in either direction.

The main limitation is that this is conditional on the supplied first-level model and equal-weight group inference. Between-participant variation is used for uncertainty because participant variance maps are unavailable. The common-support mask prevents out-of-coverage zeros from entering as observations, but conclusions apply only to that intersection and the stated upstream model. Preprocessing and first-level modeling were not repeated. The scientific outcome and assessment above were evaluated from the unchanged complete delivery; shipped results were not treated as replay evidence.
