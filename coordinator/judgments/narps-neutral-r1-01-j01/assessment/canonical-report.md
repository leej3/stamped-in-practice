## Scientific assessment

The group analysis is appropriate for the supplied stage and question. Its one-sample equal-weight random-effects *t* model uses 54 independent participant-level estimates (df = 53); it does not treat COPEs as standardized statistics. IDs match the 54-person equal-indifference cohort. The analysis checks common dimensions and affines, and constructs a standard-MNI brain mask intersected with nonzero support across all participants. No one was excluded. This makes the common-support limit inspectable, though it narrows where inference applies.

The primary test is positive one-sided sign-flip max-*T* over 9,999 permutations across 186,400 voxels, with voxelwise FWE control at .05. It meets the supplied whole-brain correction requirement. The broad MNI vmPFC core (x −15 to 15, y 20 to 55, z −20 to 5 mm) is an explicit anatomical envelope used for descriptive estimation; no specific atlas was required. The corrected peak at (−26, −102, −3.6) mm is posterior occipital rather than vmPFC. The report gives an ROI mean of 0.0018, between-participant SD 0.0629, 95% *t* interval [−0.0153, 0.0190] and one-sided uncorrected *p* = .416. It correctly does not treat this interval as an equivalence test.

The conclusion is properly limited: there is no corrected evidence supporting a positive vmPFC gain effect in these supplied maps, not proof that the effect is zero. The report also identifies that the gain modulator was centered and variance-normalized upstream, so estimates are not a response change per currency unit; that the equal-weight model cannot use absent participant variance maps; and that sign-flip inference assumes exchangeability and symmetric errors. It appropriately avoids causal claims and a gain-versus-loss comparison not supplied by this analysis.

The numerical and figure evidence is internally consistent with that interpretation. A scientific replay was not performed: the report identifies `python analysis/group_analysis.py`, but no worker-supplied environment or dependency installation instructions establish safe prerequisites. The frozen replay helper's generic byte comparator also does not implement the declared numeric comparison needed for NIfTI arrays. This leaves replay availability not-checked; it does not weaken the scientific conclusion supported by the shipped script, results, figures and report.
