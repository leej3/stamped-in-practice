## Finding

The 54 participant maps did not support a positive gain effect in the vmPFC after whole-brain correction. Bonferroni correction found 11 voxels outside the vmPFC mask; a 9,999-permutation sign-flip max-statistic sensitivity analysis found no corrected voxels. The vmPFC mean coefficient was −0.0078 (95% CI −0.0343 to 0.0187). All 54 participants were retained.

The maps contain normalized-regressor coefficients, not response-per-dollar slopes. The report discusses this and the limits of the supplied upstream model. It follows the original [NARPS whole-brain correction requirement](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf).
