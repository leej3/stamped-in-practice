## Result

**The analysis does not support a positive gain-related response in vmPFC.** The bilateral medial orbitofrontal ROI mean was −0.0079 cope (95% t interval −0.0346 to 0.0188; *n* = 54), and no voxel surviving whole-brain max-*t* FWE correction overlapped the ROI. The corrected whole-brain test found 24 positive voxels elsewhere (minimum corrected *p* = .00050). A lack of vmPFC support here does not show that the true effect is zero. The [report](</Users/johnlee/code/CON/stamped-in-practice-workers/codex/narps-r1-09/results/report.md>) explains the interpretation and limits.

The analysis follows NARPS’s whole-brain reporting instruction and uses a fixed medial orbitofrontal atlas definition to operationalize vmPFC ([NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf), [NARPS ROI discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10233921/), [AAL atlas](https://doi.org/10.1006/nimg.2001.0978)). All 54 maps passed checksum and geometry checks; no participants were excluded. Because participant variance maps were not supplied, the group test is unweighted and cannot propagate first-level uncertainty. The cope values also are not currency-unit slopes.
