## Result

The 54 participant maps do **not support** a greater vmPFC response as potential gain increases under whole-brain FWE correction. No voxel in the prespecified vmPFC search zone survived correction: its largest positive statistic was *t*(53) = 4.19 at MNI [-2, 24, -18], whole-brain FWE *p* = .778. The mean vmPFC effect was near zero (95% *t* interval [-0.0143, 0.0141] in upstream coefficient units).

The whole-brain analysis found 24 corrected voxels, with its peak outside vmPFC. That finding does not support the vmPFC hypothesis. A lack of corrected support does not establish that the neural effect is exactly zero; the supplied coefficients are not per-currency-unit effects, and participant variance maps were unavailable.
