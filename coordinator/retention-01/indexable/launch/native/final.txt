## Finding

The analysis **did not support a positive gain-related vmPFC response** under whole-brain correction. With 54 participants and 9,999 one-sided sign-flip max-T permutations, no vmPFC voxels passed FWER correction. The descriptive vmPFC mean was −0.0095 map units (95% t interval −0.0377 to 0.0187). This does not establish a zero effect: the interval includes zero, and the supplied maps do not include participant-level variance estimates.

There were 24 corrected voxels in three small posterior occipital components. They do not support the vmPFC hypothesis. The report explains the model, anatomical localization, effect-estimate scale, exclusions, and limitations.
