## Finding

The 54 participant maps **do not support a positive vmPFC gain effect after whole-brain correction**. No voxel in the predefined vmPFC reference survived correction; its peak had \(p_\mathrm{FWE}=.801\). The descriptive vmPFC mean was 0.0062 map units (95% *t* interval −0.0204 to 0.0327).

The whole-brain test found 24 corrected positive voxels in three small occipital-pole clusters. Those findings are outside vmPFC and do not support the hypothesis. The test follows the positive vmPFC hypothesis in the [original NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf). These coefficients come from a standardized gain modulator, so they are not response changes per dollar. A null corrected result is not evidence that the true effect is exactly zero.
