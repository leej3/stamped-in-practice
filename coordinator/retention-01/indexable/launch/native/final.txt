## Finding

The 54 participant maps do **not support a positive vmPFC gain effect** after whole-brain max-T family-wise correction. In the independent vmPFC sphere, the highest statistic was *t* = 1.30 (whole-brain FWE *p* = 1.00); the mean effect was −0.011, with a 95% interval of [−0.041, 0.018].

There were 24 corrected positive voxels in three small clusters outside vmPFC. The global peak was *t* = 6.62, FWE *p* = .0009. The lack of vmPFC support does not establish that the true effect is zero; it applies to these supplied estimates and the fixed upstream model. The report details the estimand, uncertainty, exclusions, and limitations. The whole-brain criterion follows the [NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf).
