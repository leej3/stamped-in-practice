## Finding

The positive vmPFC gain-effect hypothesis was **not supported** by whole-brain FWE inference. Of 24 positive voxels surviving max-t correction, none fell in the vmPFC parcels; the strongest peak was posterior occipital. The vmPFC parcel mean was −0.0079 model units (95% t interval −0.0346 to 0.0188). This is a lack of corrected evidence for the hypothesized response, not proof that the true effect is zero.

The analysis used all 54 participants, with no exclusions. One participant flagged by an outlier screen was retained; omitting them for a sensitivity summary did not change the interpretation. The report explains the model, effect-estimate units, uncertainty, and upstream limitations.
