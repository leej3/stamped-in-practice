## Scientific judgment

**Scientific adequacy: supported.** The report's conclusion of no corrected positive vmPFC support is supported by a defensible one-sided voxelwise one-sample t analysis with whole-brain sign-flip max-T familywise correction. All 54 participant maps and metadata are included, map geometry and finite coverage are checked, no outcome-based exclusions are made, and the shared nonzero support mask avoids treating missing coverage as zero. The participant is the unit of inference. The analysis uses 9,999 sign flips, a fixed seed, and checks its t-statistic formula against SciPy. Results report no max-T-FWE significant voxel in the broad, independently specified AAL3v2 medial-orbitofrontal/rectus proxy (minimum corrected p = 0.788); the proxy-level mean is negative with a confidence interval spanning zero. Whole-brain corrected positive peaks are far posterior (y = −94 to −102 mm) and are not vmPFC. This supports a lack of evidence for the stated positive vmPFC effect, not a claim that the true effect is zero.

**Hypothesis conclusion (reported outcome):** “These maps do not support a positive vmPFC response after whole-brain familywise correction.” This reported outcome is preserved separately from the adequacy label.

The anatomical proxy is broad and approximate, and the report appropriately treats it as secondary. The upstream gain regressor was normalized, so the maps do not estimate response per currency unit. Participant variance maps are absent; the equal-weight one-sample model is appropriate to the supplied participant effects but cannot account for within-participant precision. The sign-flip inference relies on symmetric-null/sign-exchangeability assumptions and applies to the common-support mask. The less stringent BH-FDR sensitivity includes one proxy voxel, but it does not replace the required whole-brain FWE criterion. A lack of corrected support does not establish no response or rule out smaller effects.

**Scientific conclusion:** no whole-brain-corrected evidence supports a positive vmPFC response in this supplied group analysis. This is the worker's stated conclusion and is supported within the model and data limits above.

**Scientific artifacts.** The report, protocol, script, and results are in `report/report.md`, `protocol/analysis-plan.md`, `code/analyze.py`, and `results/summary.json` (capture paths). Supporting checks and figures include `results/participant_qc.tsv`, `results/vmPFC_proxy_subject_values.tsv`, `results/vmPFC_proxy_summary.json`, `figures/participant_qc.png`, `figures/vmPFC_proxy_distribution.png`, `figures/positive_maxT_FWE.png`, and `figures/positive_maxT_FWE_peak.png`.
