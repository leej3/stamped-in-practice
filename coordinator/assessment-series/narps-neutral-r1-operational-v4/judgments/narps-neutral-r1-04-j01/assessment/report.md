The material inference problem is in `analysis.py`: the support mask admits a voxel when at least 90% of maps are nonzero, then `X = data[:, mask]` sends all 54 image values—including zeros in maps where the voxel is outside coverage—into the group mean, t statistic, and every sign flip. The script itself defines support as `a != 0` (`content/workspace/analysis.py`, capture SHA-256 `be9dfce22879dc38b64f8b9cc6bda8a262258c88cb8bf5a7e14d691d479f9b07`, file SHA-256 `d85798eaf62629aa12cf2783d94399860ca93ad759a5563bab73926082570920`). Thus the coverage threshold does not implement missing-data handling: zero-coded noncoverage is treated as an observed zero effect. This may attenuate or otherwise alter voxelwise estimates and the max-T reference distribution. No complete-coverage or per-voxel observed-participant sensitivity analysis is reported. Since whole-brain correction is required, the VM negative result and corrected absence cannot be accepted as established from this test. This is an assessment of inference conditional on the supplied participant maps; it does not reject the upstream modeling choices or infer the outcome under a different valid analysis.
