**Conclusion:** The supplied data do not support a positive vmPFC gain effect after whole-brain correction. No voxel in the predefined vmPFC locator survived correction; its peak had whole-brain FWE p = 0.932. Elsewhere, 24 voxels in three small posterior/inferior clusters survived correction. The analysis used whole-brain correction, as required by the [NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf).

The study includes all 54 participants, with no exclusions. All map checksums matched the manifest. The result is conditional on the supplied upstream model: these cope values use normalized-regressor units, not response change per dollar. A null corrected result is not evidence that the true vmPFC effect is zero.
