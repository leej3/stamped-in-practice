The analysis found **no whole-brain-corrected support** for a positive gain effect in the vmPFC proxy. Its mean effect was −0.008 supplied units (95% t interval −0.035 to 0.019), and no proxy voxels survived correction. The test found 23 corrected voxels in small posterior-inferior foci; their location, including one peak outside AAL3-labeled tissue, makes them difficult to interpret.

The report discusses what this lack of support can and cannot establish, the effect-map scale, group model, checks, exclusions, and limitations.
