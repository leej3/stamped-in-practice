## Result

The whole-brain-corrected analysis found **no support for an increasing vmPFC response** as potential gain increased. No significant voxels overlapped the independently defined vmPFC sphere. Its mean effect was −0.0061 COPE units (95% CI [−0.0342, 0.0221]); this does not establish that the true effect is zero.

Twenty-four positive voxels survived whole-brain max-T FWE correction, in small posterior clusters outside vmPFC. The report explains the model, effect scale, uncertainty, exclusions, and limitations.
