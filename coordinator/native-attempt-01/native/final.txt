## Conclusion

The supplied maps do **not support a positive vmPFC gain effect** in the NARPS equal-indifference group. The vmPFC sphere estimate was −0.0055 cope units (95% CI −0.0309 to 0.0199; one-sided *p* = .667), with no voxel in the sphere surviving whole-brain correction. Eleven positive voxels survived correction elsewhere, with the strongest posterior to vmPFC.

The analysis retained all 54 participants. Because the gain regressor was normalized upstream, these cope values are not BOLD change per currency unit. The report also explains why this result does not establish that the true vmPFC effect is zero.
