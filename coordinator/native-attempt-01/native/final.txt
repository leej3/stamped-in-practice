## Conclusion

The supplied maps do not support a positive vmPFC gain effect under whole-brain family-wise error correction. The vmPFC-core mean was 0.0018 contrast units (95% interval −0.0153 to 0.0190; one-sided *p* = .416). A small posterior cluster survived correction, but it was outside vmPFC.

The analysis included all 54 participants. Because the maps are normalized contrast estimates and participant variance maps were unavailable, the group model weighted participants equally; the result is not an effect per currency unit. The report discusses these and other limits.
