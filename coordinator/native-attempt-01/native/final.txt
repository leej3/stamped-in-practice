## Conclusion

This analysis **did not support a greater vmPFC response with increasing potential gain**: no positive whole-brain FWE-significant voxel overlapped the prespecified vmPFC sphere. The sphere’s mean estimate was −0.0061, with a 95% CI of −0.0342 to 0.0221 on the supplied contrast-estimate scale.

A positive whole-brain result did appear elsewhere: 24 voxels across three small posterior clusters, with maximum-statistic FWE *p* = .0006. These clusters are anatomically remote from vmPFC and do not support the stated hypothesis. This follows the [original NARPS Hypothesis 1 instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf) for a positive vmPFC effect and whole-brain analysis.
