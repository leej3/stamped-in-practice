## Conclusion

The analysis found **no whole-brain FWE-corrected support for a positive vmPFC response**. In a broad, independently defined vmPFC proxy, the mean contrast was −0.0037 (95% CI −0.0287 to 0.0212); no voxel survived whole-brain correction there (minimum corrected *p* = 0.788).

The whole-brain test did find 24 positive voxels in three small posterior clusters, far from vmPFC. That does not support the vmPFC hypothesis. A lack of corrected support does not show that the true effect is zero.
