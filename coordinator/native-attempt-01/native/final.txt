## Conclusion

The analysis did **not support** a positive gain effect in vmPFC: no vmPFC voxels survived one-sided whole-brain FWE correction. The vmPFC average was near zero, with a 95% interval spanning both directions. This is a lack of corrected evidence, not proof that the effect is absent. The test follows NARPS’s positive vmPFC hypothesis and whole-brain reporting requirement ([original instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf)).

All 54 maps passed integrity and geometry checks; none were excluded. Whole-brain correction found 24 positive voxels in three small posterior occipital clusters, outside the vmPFC mask.
