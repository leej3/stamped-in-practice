**The group analysis does not support a greater vmPFC response with increasing potential gain.** In the independent 5 mm sphere centered at MNI [2, 46, −8], the mean effect estimate was −0.012 upstream regressor units (95% t interval −0.052 to 0.028; one-sided p = .726, uncorrected). No positive vmPFC voxel survived whole-brain FWE correction. The sphere uses coordinates reported in valuation literature ([Bartra et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.02.063)); the primary test follows the positive-gain hypothesis in the [original NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf).

Across all 198,029 voxels in the common map support, 24 positive voxels survived correction; the peak was posterior, outside vmPFC (MNI [−26, −102, −3.6], FWE p = .0006). That result does not establish the vmPFC hypothesis. A lack of corrected vmPFC findings is not proof of zero effect: the interval includes modest positive and negative values.

The analysis included all 54 participants, with no exclusions. The supplied estimates use a mean-centered, variance-normalized gain regressor, so they cannot be interpreted as BOLD change per currency unit. First-level variance maps were unavailable, limiting group-level uncertainty estimates.
