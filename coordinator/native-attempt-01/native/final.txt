The analysis found **no whole-brain corrected evidence for increased gain response in vmPFC**. The vmPFC mean effect was −0.009 (95% t interval: −0.040 to 0.022), with no vmPFC voxels surviving correction. Elsewhere, 23 voxels in three small posterior occipital-pole clusters passed whole-brain max-T correction; those findings do not support the vmPFC hypothesis.

The report explains the model, vmPFC definition, exclusions, and limits of the supplied effect estimates. All 54 participants were included.
