**Conclusion:** No. The whole brain corrected analysis found no support for a positive gain effect in vmPFC. It found 24 positive voxels in three posterior clusters; the peak was *t*(53) = 6.62, max statistic FWE *p* = .001. In the vmPFC locator, the descriptive mean effect was −0.003 (95% interval −0.021 to 0.016). The conclusion follows the original instructions’ requirement for whole brain corrected decisions ([NARPS Hypothesis 1 instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf)).

I used all 54 participants, with no exclusions, and a one sample group model with 5,000 sign flips and maximum statistic correction. The supplied effects use a normalized gain regressor, so they are not estimates of BOLD change per currency unit. No participant variance maps were supplied; a null result does not establish that the true vmPFC effect is zero.
