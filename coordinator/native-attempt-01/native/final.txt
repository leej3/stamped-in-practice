## Finding

The analysis does **not support a positive gain response in vmPFC** under whole brain familywise error correction. The vmPFC zone had no corrected voxels; its peak was *t*(53) = 4.19, below the corrected threshold of 5.40. Its average estimate was −0.00048 (95% CI −0.0143 to 0.0133).

A positive effect did pass whole brain correction elsewhere: peak *t*(53) = 6.62, max-T FWE *p* = .001, with 24 significant voxels across three clusters. This does not establish the vmPFC hypothesis. The vmPFC result is not proof of a zero effect; see the report for uncertainty and limits of the supplied contrast estimates.

The analysis used all 54 participants, verified all map checksums, and applied 5,000 one-sided participant sign-flip permutations. The whole brain decision follows the [original NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf), which specify a positive vmPFC hypothesis and whole brain corrected reporting.
