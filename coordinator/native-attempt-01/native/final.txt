## Result

The analysis found **no support for a positive vmPFC gain effect** in the equal-indifference group. The vmPFC proxy’s mean effect was −0.0057 (95% *t* interval [−0.0323, 0.0210]); no voxel in the proxy survived whole-brain correction.

A positive whole-brain finding did survive correction (peak *t*(53) = 6.62, FWE *p* = .00090), but it was far posterior to vmPFC. It does not support the hypothesized vmPFC effect. The vmPFC interval includes both modest positive and negative effects, so this is not evidence that the true effect is exactly zero.
