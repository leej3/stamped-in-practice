**The data do not support a positive gain effect in vmPFC** under whole-brain correction. The vmPFC-box mean was −0.0026 COPE units (95% CI −0.0219 to 0.0167); its most positive voxel had max-T FWE p = .774. The whole-brain test found 24 corrected positive voxels in three small posterior clusters, with a peak at MNI (−26, −102, −3.6), t(53) = 6.62, FWE p = .0006. That finding is outside vmPFC and does not support the stated hypothesis. The correction follows the original NARPS instructions’ whole-brain requirement ([instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf)).
