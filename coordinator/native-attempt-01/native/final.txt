Completed the group analysis and report. The result does **not support** a positive vmPFC gain effect under whole-brain correction: 0 corrected voxels overlapped the atlas-based vmPFC proxy. The proxy mean was 0.0002 COPE (95% CI −0.0221 to 0.0225); this does not establish that the true effect is zero.

The analysis retained all 54 participants and found 9 positive voxels surviving Bonferroni whole-brain correction. Their peak was in a posterior/inferior location (MNI −26, −102, −3.6 mm), outside the vmPFC proxy. The supplied COPEs cannot be interpreted as change per unit of currency.
