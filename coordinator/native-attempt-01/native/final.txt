The analysis did **not** find support for a positive vmPFC gain effect in the equal-indifference group. No voxel in the coordinate-defined vmPFC display box survived whole-brain FWE correction; its mean contrast was −0.0035 (95% CI −0.0229 to 0.0160, in arbitrary upstream units). This does not establish that the effect is absent.

Elsewhere, 24 voxels in three small posterior islands survived one-sided maximum-t FWE correction. The strongest was at MNI (−26, −102, −3.6), *t*(53) = 6.62, *p*FWE = .0006. That finding does not support the vmPFC hypothesis. The estimates come from a normalized gain modulator, so they cannot be interpreted as a response change per dollar.
