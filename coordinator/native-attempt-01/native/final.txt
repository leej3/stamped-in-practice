## Finding

The whole-brain-corrected analysis did **not** support a positive gain effect in vmPFC. The vmPFC sphere’s mean estimate was −0.013 map units (95% *t* interval: −0.044 to 0.018), and none of its voxels survived correction. A small posterior cluster did survive whole-brain max-*t* correction, so the result is specific to the vmPFC hypothesis—not evidence that no positive effect exists anywhere.
