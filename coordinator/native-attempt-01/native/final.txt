## Conclusion

The supplied data do **not support a positive gain effect in vmPFC** for the equal-indifference group. The mean vmPFC estimate was −0.0036 map units (95% CI −0.0285 to 0.0212; one-sided *p* = .615; *n* = 54). No whole-brain FWE-significant voxels overlapped the anatomical vmPFC mask.

The one-sided whole-brain test used voxelwise Bonferroni correction, as required by the [NARPS Hypothesis 1 instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf). It found 11 significant voxels in three small posterior occipital foci, outside vmPFC. This is not evidence for the target hypothesis. The null vmPFC result does not prove absence of a response or establish equivalence to zero.
