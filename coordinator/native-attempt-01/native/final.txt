## Result

The analysis does **not support a positive gain effect in vmPFC**. In the anatomical vmPFC proxy, the mean effect was 0.0027 model units (95% CI −0.0217 to 0.0270; one-sided sign-flip *p* = .403; *n* = 54). No whole-brain FWE-significant voxels overlapped the proxy.

The whole-brain test found 23 positive FWE-significant voxels in three small posterior occipital clusters. Those findings do not support the vmPFC hypothesis. As the report explains, lack of support does not establish that the true effect is zero.
